# Murali Veeraragoo

Murali is the Lead AI Specialist in Standard Bank’s Corporate and Investment Banking division, where he drives AI innovation and helps teams across the business adopt generative AI. He holds a BSc in Electrical Engineering and a Master’s in Biomedical Engineering, a combination that grounds his work in both rigorous technical thinking and real-world problem solving. Before joining the bank, he spent two years as a Senior AI Developer at the CSIR, building AI solutions in a research-driven environment. He joined Standard Bank as an AI Specialist in 2024 and stepped into the lead role in April 2026, known for rapid prototyping that turns promising ideas into working tools quickly. Based in Johannesburg, Murali is focused on scaling the impact of AI across the organisation as he grows into broader leadership.
