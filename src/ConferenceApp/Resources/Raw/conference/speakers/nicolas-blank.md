# Nicolas Blank

Nicolas Blank is the founder and CTO of NBConsult, a division of Cyberlogic. He helps organisations connect technology decisions with business outcomes, with a focus on Microsoft cloud technologies, identity, Zero Trust security and AI trust.

A Microsoft Certified Master, Nicolas has been recognised as a Microsoft Most Valuable Professional (MVP) for 21 consecutive years and holds dual MVP awards in Microsoft Office Apps and Services and Microsoft Azure.

He combines deep technical expertise with a practical understanding of how organisations adopt and govern technology. Nicolas contributed to the Microsoft Zero Trust Adoption Framework and speaks internationally on cloud security, data sovereignty, AI governance and responsible AI adoption.
