# Wesley Sing

Wesley Sing is the founder of Appetite Technology, a software and AI company in Durban that has traded since 2012. He started out in broadcast and has been building software for 17 years, across more than 240 projects. He leads the engineering on Naya CRM, an agentic CRM in which an AI assistant researches leads and drafts outreach that a person approves before anything is sent. The same assistant powers Appetite Host, ChERP for churches, Newbi for digital business cards and the Kwalifi learning platform.
