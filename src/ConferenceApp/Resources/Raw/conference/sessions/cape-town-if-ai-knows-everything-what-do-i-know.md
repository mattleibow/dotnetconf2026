# If AI Knows Everything, What Do I Know?

AI can write code, explain concepts and answer questions in seconds, and for many beginners, juniors and students that makes the old feeling of "I don't belong here" louder than ever.

If the machine knows everything, what's left for me?

This talk is for the ones still learning. Drawing on my own journey as a student and a Python tutor, I'll talk honestly about how imposter syndrome shows up in the age of AI, and why it isn't a sign you're behind.

You'll leave with a few practical ways to keep trusting your own thinking, give yourself credit for what you already know, and use AI as a tool without letting it quietly shrink your confidence.

Key takeaways:

Feeling behind in the AI era is common, and it isn't proof you don't belong.

Using AI to learn is different from letting AI replace your thinking.

A simple teach-back habit for testing and trusting what you actually know.

Permission to start from where you are, without having to know everything.
