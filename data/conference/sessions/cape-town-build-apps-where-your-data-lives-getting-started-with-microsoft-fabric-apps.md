# Build Apps Where Your Data Lives: Getting Started with Microsoft Fabric Apps

What if you could build an application right where your data already lives?

Microsoft Fabric is widely known as an end-to-end data and analytics platform, but there’s another exciting side to Fabric: the ability to build data-driven applications that bring users, business logic, and insights closer to the data.

In this session, we’ll explore Microsoft Fabric Apps and see how developers and data professionals can turn data and insights into interactive experiences without creating unnecessary layers between the application and the data.

Through a practical demo, we’ll look at the building blocks of a Fabric App, how it fits into the broader Fabric platform, and scenarios where developing close to the data can simplify your architecture and accelerate delivery.

Whether you’re a .NET developer curious about Microsoft Fabric or a data professional interested in building applications, this session will give you a practical introduction and hopefully inspire you to build your first Fabric App.
