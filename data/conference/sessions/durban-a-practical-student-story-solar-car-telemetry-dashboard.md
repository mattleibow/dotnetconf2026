# A Practical Student Story: Solar Car Telemetry Dashboard

What happens when classroom knowledge meets real-world engineering, renewable energy, and the challenge of racing a solar-powered car across South Africa?

Meet Sol Invictus Dynamics, the multidisciplinary student team behind SOL-1, a solar-electric vehicle that competed in the 2026 Sasol Solar Challenge.

In this session, we take you behind the scenes of Belgium Campus iTversity's journey, exploring how students transformed software development concepts into a practical telemetry solution designed to support a high-performance solar racing team.

Discover how real-time data collection, telemetry, data visualisation, and intelligent monitoring come together to provide insights into vehicle performance, energy efficiency, and race strategy.

We'll explore the technical challenges of developing a dashboard for a moving vehicle, working with live data, presenting meaningful information, and building software that supports critical decisions under real-world conditions.

More than just a technical demonstration, this is a story of student innovation, multidisciplinary collaboration, and learning beyond the classroom — showcasing what happens when aspiring developers are given the opportunity to solve genuine engineering challenges.

From classroom code to race-day decisions — this is software development in action!
