# Your AI Agent Has Database Access. Now What?

What if your AI assistant could access your database—and one carefully crafted prompt could convince it to expose sensitive data? As AI agents gain the ability to query databases, call APIs, and execute tools, security can no longer stop at the prompt. In this talk, we'll explore real-world AI security risks, including prompt injection and unauthorised tool use, and demonstrate how layered guardrails, permission controls, and input validation can help build safer, more reliable AI systems.
