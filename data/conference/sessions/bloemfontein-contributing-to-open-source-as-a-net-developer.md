# Contributing to open source as a .NET developer

Many .NET developers sometimes miss out on the fact that because .NET is open source, they too can contribute. The runtime, the libraries, the compiler, the tooling you use every day — it's all out in the open, built by thousands of contributors, and it's waiting for you to contribute too.

Inspired by conversations from various online communities, this talk makes the case for why contributing is one of the best moves a .NET developer can make. Contributing is good for your skills, your visibility, and the community you're part of.

In this session, we'll also discuss how to find a welcoming .NET project for that first contribution, how to spot a genuine "good first issue," how to set up and make your change, and how to open a pull request without the fear that stops most people before they start.

You'll leave with an understanding of why .NET being open source matters and how to be a community member by contributing to projects.
