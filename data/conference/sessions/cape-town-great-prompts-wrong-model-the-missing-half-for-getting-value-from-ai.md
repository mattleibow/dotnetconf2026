# Great Prompts, Wrong Model: The Missing Half for Getting Value from AI

Better prompts get you better results, but only up to a ceiling. The model and tooling you choose sets that ceiling.

A new model lands every few weeks, and each has different strengths, speeds and costs. A lot of developers pick a couple of favourites and use them for everything, or let the auto router choose for them. That means overpaying for easy work and underperforming on more specialized tasks, no matter how well the prompt is written.

Here’s the uncomfortable part: everyone has access to AI now, so access is no longer an advantage. Your competitive advantage isn’t AI. It’s knowing which AI and which tools to use where. The developers and teams who match the right model to the right job, and keep that knowledge current, will cut their AI bill and ship faster than those who don’t.

In this session I’ll show how to pair good prompting with good model and tool selection, and treat models like a toolbox:

1. Why prompt tuning is necessary but not enough

2. The right model for the job. Including a practical map of today’s landscape, covering which models are strongest at which tasks, and why.

3. Multi-model workflows: use a heavyweight model to plan, a cheaper one to execute and another to review, so you cut costs and ship faster without cutting quality.

4. A decision framework for choosing a model for any task

5. How to stay current without drowning in hype. The habits, sources and quick tests I use to evaluate new releases within hours of launch

You’ll leave with a repeatable way to choose models, a sense of where the money is being wasted, and a system for staying ahead, so the prompting skills you’ve already built are working with the right model.

Level: Beginner to intermediate. No C# required.
