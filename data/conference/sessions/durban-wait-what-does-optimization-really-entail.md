# Wait, what does Optimization really entail?

We hear a lot about AI and neural networks, and we might have encountered the fact that optimization is crucial to the state-of-the-art performance of deep neural nets, but what is optimization really about and how does it work?

This presentation will unpack: what exactly is optimized during the optimization process, what is the most important traditional optimization algorithm, what are some of its benefits and limitations, what are some other optimization algorithms that have been designed to address some of the drawbacks faced, and what kinds of everyday models that we work with use optimization?
