# A Case Study in Sovereignty: The Illusion of Control

Your data is sovereign: It's in the right country and the regulator seems happy. What could possibly go wrong?

Does sovereign mean safe, does sovereign mean protected or does sovereign mean something real?

Consider: your American parent company wants everyone in its US tenant, or an attacker halfway around the world accesses your supposedly sovereign data or a drone takes out the cloud region holding your only copy. We haven’t even spoken about the agents you may want, the agentic attacks you don’t want and 20-year-old vulnerabilities that "suddenly" crop up.

Through three real-world case studies involving regulated AI adoption, cross-border acquisitions and my own company's AI deployments, we'll examine what Microsoft 365, Azure and Entra ID actually let you control, what you're trusting Microsoft to do, and what happens when regulation and reality collide.

In this session there will be awkward questions and some uncomfortable answers and the occasional discussion about Zero Trust and Atom Bombs. For real! You'll leave knowing how to identify what must remain under your control, where your dependencies lie, and how to prove your sovereignty controls are meaningful beyond a compliance audit.

Key takeaways

- Understand where physical data residency ends and meaningful digital sovereignty begins.
- Learn how to make architectural decisions within the technical, legal and operational limits of cloud sovereignty, beyond considering only the data processing argument.
- Learn a practical risk-based approach to testing and defending sovereignty decisions no matter where in the world your data may be.
