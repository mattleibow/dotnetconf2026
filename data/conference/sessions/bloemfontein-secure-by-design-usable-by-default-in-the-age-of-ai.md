# Secure by design, usable by default, in the age of AI

AI is changing what our products can do. Assistants draft, summarise, recommend and increasingly act on people’s behalf. That opens up a huge opportunity to build software that feels effortless and genuinely helpful. The teams that win will be the ones whose AI features people trust enough to actually use.

This session shows how to build that trust from both sides. It brings together the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, the OWASP Top 10:2025 and Nielsen Norman Group’s usability heuristics. Together they give practical patterns for AI features that are safe and a pleasure to use.

• AI tools that act with the user’s permissions and nothing more
• Interfaces that show what the AI is doing and why
• People approving the actions that matter, with easy undo
• Defences against prompt injection that don’t get in the way

Each pattern comes with concrete .NET code using Microsoft.Extensions.AI, plus a matching design move.

We’ll also look at the 2026 Hugging Face incident and the encouraging lesson it holds: AI is just as powerful on defence. AI-assisted monitoring raised the first alert, and defensive agents can triage, investigate and help teams respond at machine speed while people stay in control.

You’ll leave with a simple guiding principle, “friction is a budget, spend it where the risk is”, lightweight research methods for AI features, and an eleven-question checklist your team can use from the next sprint.

Audience: developers, designers, QA and product managers building with AI.
