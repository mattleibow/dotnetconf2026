# Bringing the Edge Into Focus

I previously built a digital microscope that combined IoT and AI into something useful, practical, and centred on accessibility. It could see, read, and talk, and every one of those abilities was powered by the cloud. This session is the journey of moving all of it from cloud hosted services to local edge AI, with .NET 11.

Join me as we take every capability off the cloud and onto the device itself. Vision, text recognition, speech, conversational AI, and even retrieval, all shifting from hosted services to models running locally, with no connection required. We will run the same solution across a range of low cost edge devices, from hobbyist boards to consumer AI hardware you can buy today. Along the way we will explore what each move from cloud to edge costs, where running locally surprises you, and what living on the device makes possible that the cloud never could.

Best of all, this is all still done with .NET and Avalonia, and you will get a sneak peek at the latest Avalonia features along the way.

Going from cloud to edge means this accessibility solution now travels with me, signal or not. Hopefully the session sparks some ideas about what else becomes possible when you move the intelligence onto the device.
