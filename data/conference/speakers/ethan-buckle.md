# Ethan Buckle

I’m passionate about optimisation and finding the smarter way to get the most out of any system, whether that’s a robot, a piece of software or the AI tools we all use every day.

I’ve been in the world of coding for 11 years, from world-class robots in Java to commercial software in Python, TypeScript and Rust. Today I help developers and non-developers get far more out of AI, by choosing the right tools for each job and keeping up as the landscape changes every few weeks.

It all started in competitive robotics, and that’s where I learned how to work. 11 years in competitive robotics, as a competitor and now a mentor, taught me to keep iterating, measure what actually improves performance and never settle for good enough. I founded Texpand in 2020, and we became 2024 FIRST Tech Challenge World Champions as part of the winning alliance, five-time South African national champions, and winners of the international Think Award for engineering rigour. Through our outreach programmes, which I led, we’ve introduced over 1000 students to engineering and robotics.

I bring the same approach to AI. I follow the landscape closely, and I train people on matching the best toolsets and models to each task, and building habits that keep their skills ahead of the curve.

I’m the founder of StateSync, which helps developers cut the cost of running AI coding agents, and I’m studying Mechanical and Mechatronics Engineering at UCT.
