# Sromana Mukhopadhyay

Sromana Mukhopadhyay is a co-founder and Principal Researcher at BlockQuest Labs Inc., where she works on evaluation and reliability of LLM and agentic systems. Her research focuses on benchmark construction, evidence-grounded verification, tool-use and retrieval evaluation, temporal robustness, provenance, calibration and abstention, and reproducible evaluation methodology.

She has served as a reviewer for multiple NeurIPS 2026 workshops as well as the REALM Workshop at EMNLP 2026. Earlier in her career, she conducted semiconductor reliability research at NJIT, IISER Kolkata, and ON Semiconductor, resulting in peer-reviewed publications in ECS Transactions and IEEE ICSICT. She was also co-founder and COO of ShufflUp, a machine-learning-powered cryptocurrency trading startup accelerated by StartupYard, Czech Repubplic.

Previous speaking includes the ECS Meeting in San Diego (2016), SIX Group Blockchain Conference (Zug, Switzerland, 2018), and internal technical meetups at ON Semiconductor(South Portland, Maine).
