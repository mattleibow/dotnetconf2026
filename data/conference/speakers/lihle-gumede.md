# Lihle Gumede

Thembelihle 'Lihle' Gumede is a Biomedical Technologist, Data Scientist, and Health Innovator with over 10 years of experience. Currently serving as the Community Lead for Neo4j in Southern Africa, Lihle is dedicated to bridging the gap between global graph technology and local health innovation. Through initiatives like the Biocollaborator community and the BioNexus AMR project, Lihle focuses on utilizing Knowledge Graphs and GraphRAG to solve complex data challenges and improve AI accuracy within the health sector.
